How it works

  1. Fill your profile and tick "Subscribe to scholarship alerts" when you check eligibility.
  2. Our engine automatically scans government portals (NSP, AICTE, NTA, scholarships.gov.in) for new schemes.
  3. When a new matching scholarship is found, you are alerted instantly — no need to check portals yourself.
  4. Come back here any time and enter your email to see all notifications in one place.

📌 Alerts are sent to the email you provide. For offline / demo mode, notifications are printed in the server terminal window.
